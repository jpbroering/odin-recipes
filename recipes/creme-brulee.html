Creme Brulee

Description

Crème brûlée is a dessert consisting of a rich custard base topped with a layer of hardened caramelized sugar. The custard base is generally flavored with vanilla in French cuisine, but can have other flavorings.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup heavy cream
  • 3 tablespoons white sugar
  • 3 large egg yolks
  • ¼ teaspoon vanilla extract
  • 2 tablespoons white sugar, divided

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C)
  2. Whisk together cream and 3 tablespoons sugar in a microwave-safe bowl until combined
  3. Cook in the microwave until warm, 1 to 2 minutes
  4. whisk again to dissolve sugar
  5. Whisk in egg yolks and vanilla until smooth
  6. Pour cream mixture into 2 ramekins
  7. set the ramekins in a roasting pan
  8. Pour enough hot water into the pan to reach halfway up the sides of the ramekins
  9. Bake in the preheated oven until custard is set but slightly jiggly in the center when shaken, about 50 minutes
  10. Remove ramekins from hot water and chill in the refrigerator until cold, at least 2 hours
  11. Sprinkle 1 tablespoon sugar evenly over the top of each dessert
  12. Use a kitchen torch to melt and lightly toast sugar topping until brown and bubbly, about 30 seconds
  13. Let sugar topping cool to harden before serving
