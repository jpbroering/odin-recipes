Pudim

Description

Brazilian-style flan, known locally as pudim de leite condensado, is a rich, creamy, and silky-smooth baked custard topped with a glossy, golden-amber caramel sauce.

Ingredients

  • 1 cup white sugar
  • 4 eggs, separated
  • 1 (14 ounce) can sweetened condensed milk
  • ¾ cup milk, plus
  • 2 tablespoons milk

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven to 350 degrees F (175 degrees C)
  2. Melt sugar in a heavy saucepan over low heat, stirring constantly
  3. Cook until sugar becomes a golden brown syrup, about 10 minutes
  4. Pour immediately into a round baking dish
  5. Swirl the backing dish, so caramel syrup coats the sides of the dish
  6. Set aside to cool
  7. Place egg yolks into a blender and blend on medium speed for 5 minutes
  8. Add condensed milk, 3/4 cup plus 2 tablespoons milk, and egg whites
  9. Continue to blend until well combined
  10. Pour over hardened caramel syrup in the baking dish
  11. Cover the dish with aluminum foil
  12. Line a roasting pan with a damp kitchen towel
  13. Place the baking dish on the towel inside the roasting pan
  14. Place the roasting pan on the oven rack
  15. Fill the roasting pan with boiling water to reach halfway up the sides of the baking dish
  16. Bake in the preheated oven until a knife inserted 1 inch from the edge comes out clean, 45 to 50 minutes
  17. Allow flan to cool before unmolding onto a plate
