Carbonara

Description

Carbonara is a Roman dish made with eggs, hard cheese, and cured pork. Its signature rich and silky sauce comes from beaten eggs tossed with hot pasta. The trick to making carbonara is making sure the pasta is hot enough to cook the eggs, but not so hot that they curdle.

Ingredients

  • 2 teaspoons olive oil
  • 1 pound guanctiale (cured pork cheek), diced
  • 1 (16 ounce) package spaghetti
  • 3 large eggs
  • 10 tablespoons grated Pecorino Romano cheese, divided
  • salt and freshly ground black pepper to taste

Steps

  1. Heat olive oil in a pan at medium heat
  2. Cook the guanciale until it's crisp and brown (5 to 10 minutes), occasionally mixing
  3. Drain the guanciale on paper towel
  4. Cook the spaguetti in salted water until it's tender yet firm to bite (about 9 minutes)
  5. Take a cup of the cooking water
  6. Drain the pasta
  7. Reheat the guanciale in the pan at medium heat
  8. Add the pasta to the pan
  9. Add half a cup of cooking water to the pan
  10. Mix occasionally until the water evaporate
  11. Add the other half of cup of cooking water to the pan
  12. Turn of the heat when most of the water evaporate and let it cool
  13. Add in a bowl 3 egg yolks
  14. Add fine grated pecorino romano cheese to the bowl
  15. Add black pepper to taste to the bowl
  16. Mix the ingredients until it's creamy
  17. Add the mixture to the pasta stirring quickly, until it's creamy
  18. In a plate, finish the carbonara with grated cheese and black papper on top
